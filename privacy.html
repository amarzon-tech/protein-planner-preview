Protein Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

We collect no data

Protein Planner does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your profile choice, weight, protein target, and today's foods are saved only in this device's local storage. Nothing leaves the device. Clear it in More → Clear saved plan, or by clearing site data.

No network use

The app works offline after load and does not send anything over the internet.

Medical disclaimer

Protein Planner is for education only and is not medical advice. Talk to your clinician or dietitian about your own targets.
