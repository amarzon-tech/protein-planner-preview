Protein Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

We collect no data

Protein Planner does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your profile choice, weight, protein target, and the foods on today's plan are saved only in your device's local storage so the app can remember them. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it with "Clear saved plan" in Settings, or by deleting the app / site data.

No network use

The app works offline and does not send anything over the internet after it is loaded.

Medical disclaimer

Protein Planner is for education only and is not medical advice. Protein and mineral needs are individual. Talk to your clinician or renal dietitian about your own targets.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/amarzon-tech/protein-planner.
